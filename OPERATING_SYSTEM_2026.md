# FinanceMeta Talent Network Operating System — 2026

## Position

FinanceMeta is a finance, economics, markets, quantitative-research, and fintech talent network. It should be distinct from a general builder community: every flagship program should connect members to finance/economics learning, research, market analysis, fintech building, competitions, professors, employers, or other externally reviewable outcomes.

This document defines operating infrastructure. It does not claim that target membership, chapter, sponsor, professor, publication, or internship outcomes have already happened.

## Program portfolio

### Labs

Purpose: small research teams working on bounded finance/economics questions.

Required output: reproducible analysis, research note, dataset/tool, working paper, or negative-result report.

Completion gate:

- explicit question and scope;
- source/data provenance;
- reproducible method;
- reviewed result;
- limitations;
- public or permissioned final artifact.

### Axiom Pathways

Purpose: guided skill pathways toward quantitative finance, economics, markets, fintech, and adjacent careers.

Required output: assessed capstone plus a documented skill map. Course/module completion alone is not the terminal outcome.

### Debrief

Purpose: concise recurring interpretation of market/economic developments.

Required output: sourced brief with facts separated from analysis and no unsupported investment-performance claims.

### FinTech Studio

Purpose: build and test finance-related software.

Required output: tested demo/product, target-user definition, documented architecture, and observed feedback/usage where available.

### Fellowship

Purpose: selective multi-week research/build experience.

Required output: reviewed capstone and an attributable contribution record.

### Investment / quantitative competition

Purpose: evaluate reasoning, research process, risk awareness, and technical execution rather than reward unsupported return claims.

Required output: auditable submission plus scoring record.

### Speaker / professor network

Purpose: connect members to practitioners and researchers for learning, review, and collaboration.

Success is measured by completed sessions/reviews/collaborations, not names in a prospect list.

## Chapter operating standard

A chapter is `ACTIVE` only if all of the following are true:

- named accountable chapter lead;
- at least one FinanceMeta-aligned activity in the trailing 30 days;
- at least one attributable participant artifact or assessed learning output in the trailing 30 days;
- next milestone is scheduled;
- chapter submits a short evidence report;
- no unresolved conduct/compliance blocker.

A chapter directory entry by itself is not an active chapter.

## Competition structure

A flagship competition should use stages that reward process quality.

1. Registration and eligibility.
2. Public rules/data/problem statement.
3. Qualification round if capacity requires it.
4. Main submission.
5. Blind or conflict-aware scoring where feasible.
6. Finalist review/presentation.
7. Published rubric and winner rationale.
8. Post-event report with participation and completion evidence.

### Default scoring rubric

| Dimension | Weight | What it rewards |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Problem framing | 15 | clarity, assumptions, relevance |
| Method / analysis | 25 | technical/economic rigor |
| Evidence | 20 | correct sourcing, validation, reproducibility |
| Risk / limitations | 15 | awareness of uncertainty and failure modes |
| Communication | 15 | clear argument and visualization |
| Originality / usefulness | 10 | non-trivial insight or implementation |

Any future live-trading or monetary-prize structure should receive separate legal/compliance review before launch.

## Professor / university partnership packet

Each partnership proposal should contain:

- one-sentence FinanceMeta position;
- specific program relevant to the recipient;
- exact requested involvement: review, talk, mentorship, judging, research feedback, opportunity sharing, or institutional collaboration;
- participant profile and selection method;
- expected time commitment;
- safeguarding/logistics contact;
- proposed deliverable or event;
- evidence/reporting plan;
- no inflated user/member claims.

## Sponsor packet structure

1. Mission and audience definition.
2. Programs with concrete participant outputs.
3. Verified reach/activity metrics with definitions and dates.
4. Specific sponsorship inventory.
5. Brand-safety and participant-data boundaries.
6. Reporting plan.
7. Custom activation options.
8. Contact and next step.

Sponsorship proposals should distinguish `verified current metrics` from `campaign targets`.

## Research publication workflow

1. Intake and question freeze.
2. Data/source audit.
3. Analysis plan and baseline definition.
4. Execution with retained provenance.
5. Internal review.
6. Reproduction/verification pass.
7. Writing and claim audit.
8. External review where possible.
9. Publish or submit.
10. Record outcome without hiding rejection, null, or negative findings.

## Newsletter structure

A useful recurring issue can contain:

- one market/economics explainer;
- one member research/build artifact;
- one sourced opportunity section;
- one upcoming FinanceMeta program/event;
- one short methods/data note.

Publication frequency should follow editorial capacity; consistency and quality matter more than daily volume.

## Annual programming skeleton

Use this as a sequencing model, not as a claim that events are scheduled.

### Q1: foundations

- member onboarding and pathway diagnostics;
- first Axiom Pathways cycle;
- Labs project intake;
- chapter lead onboarding;
- professor/reviewer recruitment.

### Q2: production

- Labs research sprint;
- FinTech Studio cohort;
- Debrief publication cadence;
- regional/chapter quantitative challenge;
- first capstone review day.

### Q3: flagship

- major investment/quantitative competition;
- professor/practitioner speaker series;
- research working-paper release cycle;
- internship/opportunity partnerships.

### Q4: proof and renewal

- annual FinanceMeta report;
- chapter evidence audit;
- strongest project/research showcase;
- partner renewal based on observed outputs;
- next-year program selection based on completion/outcome data.

## Operating metrics

### Activity

- evidence-active members in trailing 14/30 days;
- active chapter count under the evidence definition;
- active research/build teams;
- program completion rate;
- median time to first artifact.

### Quality

- reviewed capstones;
- reproducible research outputs;
- completed fintech demos;
- competition submission completion rate;
- artifacts passing reviewer rubric;
- projects with external reviewers.

### External outcomes

- verified professor/reviewer engagements;
- external submissions and results;
- published research/technical notes;
- opportunity placements attributable to FinanceMeta;
- external adoption or users of fintech projects;
- institutional/sponsor relationships that actually executed an activity.

Do not report registrations, social impressions, mailing-list size, or chapter listings as active-member outcomes without a separate definition.

## Portfolio disposition

Every quarter, assign each program/project one status:

- `SCALE`: strong completion and outcome evidence;
- `CONTINUE`: healthy but not yet ready to scale;
- `FIX`: meaningful value but a clear operational/evidence failure;
- `FREEZE`: useful idea, low current priority;
- `ARCHIVE`: completed or retired;
- `STOP`: no longer justifies resources.

New programs should not be launched merely to avoid stopping weak ones.

## Integrity standard

For any public FinanceMeta statement such as “active members,” “chapters,” “research publications,” “professors involved,” “internships,” or “competition participants,” the organization must be able to show a definition, source record, and verification date. Targets are welcome, but targets remain labeled as targets until evidence exists.
