# FinanceMeta — September 2026 Command Center

## Monthly objective

September converts FinanceMeta from program inventory and warm external signals into **delivered, auditable outcomes**. Work is prioritized by the evidence hierarchy in `OPERATING_SYSTEM_2026.md`: implementation is useful, but September wins require external participation and completed outcomes wherever possible.

## September scoreboard

| Lane | Sep 30 target | Evidence required |
|---|---|---|
| FinTech Studio Buildathon | event logistics frozen; sponsor/data support used in a delivered or launch-ready program | public event page, rules, registrations, API/data terms, delivery report |
| HAVEN research-quality contribution | planning call completed; rubric/template locked; seminar delivery path frozen | meeting notes, artifact version, seminar date, participant-use record |
| Jump$tart resource path | one qualifying FinanceMeta resource audited and submission-ready | criteria map, citations, advice-risk audit, usability review, receipt when submitted |
| Investment / Quant Challenge | rules and judging frozen before submissions | rules, anti-lookahead policy, rubric, conflict policy, judging record |
| Labs / FI-JEPA | one research lane promoted on evidence, not novelty | protocol, provenance, matched baselines, retained results, review decision |
| Chapters | active chapters measured by output, not group size | trailing-45-day artifact/event evidence |
| Public reporting | one September evidence report | source-linked metrics, failures, external contributions, next actions |

## Week 1 — Freeze and instrument (Sep 1–7)

- Freeze the Buildathon dates, team model, eligibility, judging, data-use rules, and required public page.
- Create one sponsor logistics packet. Do not repeatedly contact a sponsor until the requested URLs/dates are stable.
- Freeze the Investment/Quant Challenge rulebook before opening submissions.
- Select the single FinanceMeta educational resource most likely to satisfy Jump$tart-style listing criteria and run a line-by-line standards audit.
- Rank Labs/FI-JEPA work by evidence maturity. No new research lane gets compute until its question, baseline, data provenance, primary metric, and failure condition are written.
- Start the September evidence ledger: every registration, completed artifact, reviewer contribution, sponsor deliverable, failure, and complaint must have a source.

## Week 2 — Launch and externalize (Sep 8–14)

- Publish the stable Buildathon/event surface only after logistics are frozen.
- Recruit teams through chapters, schools, universities, and existing community channels; track registrations separately from active teams.
- Send reviewers/judges one bounded packet: role, deadline, rubric, conflict policy, expected workload.
- Prepare the Sep 15 HAVEN planning call packet: agenda, exact requested rubric/template contribution, seminar objective, participant output to be reviewed, attribution permission question.
- Run the first research evidence review on FI-JEPA/Labs work; projects can end as positive, negative, inconclusive, or archived.

## Week 3 — Deliver and validate (Sep 15–21)

- Hold the HAVEN planning call and record decisions, owners, dates, and the exact external contribution promised.
- Convert sponsor support into participant-visible utility; sponsorship is not counted as a delivered outcome until teams actually use the support or the event is executed.
- Conduct at least one external review of a Labs/FI-JEPA artifact using the 28-point research rubric.
- Audit chapter status using the 45-day output rule and classify each chapter ACTIVE / AT RISK / DORMANT / CLOSED.
- Produce an interim evidence report: registrations, active builders, completed artifacts, external reviewers, sponsor contributions used, blockers.

## Week 4 — Close loops (Sep 22–30)

- Execute or freeze the final Buildathon delivery package and preserve outcomes by team/project.
- Deliver or lock the post-Sep-25 HAVEN seminar contribution and attach the external rubric/template to the participant workflow.
- Submit only the educational resource that passes the standards/advice-risk/usability audit; preserve the receipt and reviewer outcome.
- Close weak research lanes instead of keeping them indefinitely "active".
- Publish the September evidence report with exact counting rules and links.

## Operating rules

1. Registrations are not completions.
2. Sponsor interest is not sponsor utilization.
3. A promised reviewer is not an external contribution until review happens.
4. A chapter is not active because a chat group exists.
5. Synthetic or paper-trading results are never represented as realized market performance.
6. No rule, metric, threshold, baseline, or eligibility criterion changes after outcomes are inspected unless the change begins a new version.
7. Public claims must be evidence-level tagged internally before publication.

## Daily command loop

Each day ends with five lines:

- **SHIPPED:** artifacts/events actually completed today.
- **EXTERNAL:** new external participation or outcome with source.
- **METRIC:** one source-backed movement in the scoreboard.
- **BLOCKER:** the single highest-leverage unresolved blocker.
- **NEXT:** the one action that most increases E3/E4 evidence tomorrow.

The September goal is not maximum activity. It is a smaller set of programs with stronger proof.
