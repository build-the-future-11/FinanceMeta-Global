# FinanceMeta 12-Week Researcher Program

## Objective

Produce small numbers of reviewable finance/economics/fintech artifacts with strong methods and reproducibility. Enrollment volume is not a success metric.

## Team shape

Each active project has:
- 1 DRI;
- 1–3 contributors;
- 1 reviewer not responsible for the primary implementation;
- optional domain adviser.

One person may contribute to multiple projects, but should have only one primary DRI role at a time.

## Entry gate

A participant becomes ACTIVE only after:
1. choosing one bounded lane;
2. accepting the research/release standard;
3. receiving one first artifact with a due date;
4. receiving a named reviewer;
5. producing the first reviewable artifact.

Form completion alone is not activation.

## Weeks 1–2 — question and reproduction

Outputs:
- research question;
- null;
- literature/background note;
- baseline reproduction;
- first data/provenance manifest;
- initial failure-mode list.

Gate: reproduce something simple before proposing complexity.

## Weeks 3–4 — experiment contract

Outputs:
- frozen protocol;
- split/timestamp rules;
- metrics;
- baseline matrix;
- search budget;
- seed policy;
- falsification checks;
- claim boundary.

Gate: reviewer signs pre-result readiness.

## Weeks 5–7 — implementation

Outputs:
- runnable code;
- tests;
- environment lock;
- deterministic fixture;
- train/validation evidence;
- no confirmatory held-out access unless explicitly unlocked.

Gate: another contributor can reproduce the baseline path.

## Weeks 8–9 — evaluation

After reviewer unlock:
- run frozen held-out evaluation once under the contract;
- retain all seeds/regimes;
- run prespecified falsification checks;
- do not alter the protocol based on unfavorable outcomes.

## Weeks 10–11 — audit and writing

Outputs:
- raw result tables;
- failure analysis;
- limitations;
- claim-state decision;
- reviewer reproduction note;
- draft research note/report.

## Week 12 — release decision

Choose:
- RELEASE;
- NEGATIVE RESULT RELEASE;
- REVISE;
- HOLD;
- ARCHIVE.

A project is not required to be positive to complete the program.

## Weekly cadence

### Monday
DRI posts:
- one outcome for the week;
- current SHA;
- blocker;
- reviewer needed.

### Midweek
Reviewer checks evidence, not polish.

### Weekend
Project records:
- artifact produced;
- reproduction status;
- claim-state changes;
- next gate.

## Capacity rule

Stop accepting additional active researchers when:
- a project lacks a reviewer;
- reviews are more than one cycle behind;
- current WIP exceeds five research lanes;
- existing participants lack bounded artifacts.

Waitlist instead of pretending everyone is active.

## Completion evidence

A participant is "completed" only with a traceable contribution to an artifact that passed its project's release decision. Attendance, Discord membership, application, or title does not count by itself.

## Authorship and credit

Credit follows actual intellectual, technical, analysis, writing, or review contribution. Maintain a contribution ledger. Do not grant authorship solely for enrollment, supervision title, or organizational seniority.
