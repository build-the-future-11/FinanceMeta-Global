# FinanceMeta Internal Position — November 1, 2026 Stock Pitch Competition

**Status:** FINANCEMETA INTERNAL POSITION — NOT PARTNER-APPROVED  
**Source of truth for partner decisions:** Empiric Pitch operating brief v1.0, August 17, 2026  
**Purpose of this file:** Give FinanceMeta a concrete, auditable negotiating position without pretending Empiric Investing, Youth Economy Lab, or USAEO has approved unresolved rules.

## Fixed boundaries from the operating brief

The following are treated as existing constraints, not FinanceMeta inventions:

- Registration and the participant workflow remain on the Empiric Pitch platform.
- Empiric owns platform/live operations and the judging infrastructure, subject to partner sign-off in the brief.
- The competition opens November 1, 2026 and runs for one month.
- Promotion begins only after the separate September Moneyplotter competition closes.
- The scoring model proposed in the brief weights reasoning and research over presentation.
- All four partners must sign off on competition details before promotion.
- The September Moneyplotter event is separate; FinanceMeta is only a distribution partner there.

## FinanceMeta recommendations

These are positions to take into partner sign-off. They are not final rules until the partner brief is updated and redistributed.

### 1. Entry fee: free for the first year

**Recommendation:** free entry.

Reasoning:

- reduces access friction
- makes global student participation easier
- avoids revenue-split and payment-custody complexity
- keeps the first cycle focused on judging quality, platform integrity, and participation

If another partner proposes a fee, require the brief to name the entity collecting funds, refund policy, financial responsibility, and revenue allocation before promotion.

### 2. Entry format: individual

**Recommendation:** individual entries for v1.

Reasoning:

- simplifies authorship attribution
- aligns cleanly with blind judging
- avoids ambiguous contribution splits
- reduces integrity-review complexity

Team support can be revisited in a later cycle after the single-author workflow is validated.

### 3. Eligibility: high school and undergraduate, worldwide where operationally supported

**Recommendation:** retain the brief's high-school and undergraduate boundary and use worldwide eligibility only where the platform and partners can operationally support registration, privacy, and participant support.

Do not advertise "worldwide" until Empiric confirms the registration workflow supports the intended jurisdictions.

### 4. Memo cap: 2,000 words

**Recommendation:** 2,000 words for the main memo, excluding citations and system-generated appendices.

Required sections:

1. thesis
2. business overview and key drivers
3. financial analysis
4. valuation
5. catalysts
6. risks / disconfirming evidence
7. sources

This is long enough for real analysis while keeping judge workload bounded.

### 5. Scoring: preserve the proposed 30/25/20/15/10 structure

| Dimension | Weight |
| --- | ---: |
| Thesis and reasoning | 30% |
| Financial analysis and valuation | 25% |
| Evidence and citations | 20% |
| Risk assessment | 15% |
| Structure and writing | 10% |

Do not add portfolio return or subsequent stock performance to the score. The competition is framed as equity-research quality, and future market movement would add look-ahead noise rather than measure the quality of the submitted reasoning.

### 6. Authenticity and AI boundary

Keep the brief's core rule:

- work must be authored inside Empiric Pitch
- paste events and authoring history remain auditable
- editing/proofreading assistance may be used only if disclosed
- substantially AI-generated submissions are not eligible for normal scoring

Clarify before launch what "substantially AI generated" means operationally. Disqualification should require review of the authoring evidence, not a standalone AI detector score.

### 7. Submission-time evidence boundary

The submission is judged as the argument that existed at the deadline.

- editing locks at the deadline
- no post-deadline changes
- judges evaluate citations and claims against information available to the participant by the submission deadline
- later market performance does not retroactively improve or weaken the research score

This prevents hindsight from contaminating the evaluation.

### 8. Blind judging and conflicts

FinanceMeta should support:

- author identity, school, and country hidden from judges during scoring
- judges disclosing direct relationships with a participant or material conflicts involving a submission
- recusal when a conflict could reasonably affect impartial scoring
- calibration material before scoring begins
- at least two independent scores per memo when judge capacity permits
- a third review for finalists or material scoring disagreement when capacity permits

No partner should claim a judge headcount until named judges have actually confirmed.

### 9. Prizes

No cash-prize amount should appear in public copy until the funding source and responsible entity are confirmed in writing.

FinanceMeta position:

- free entry does not depend on a cash prize
- sponsor-funded cash is acceptable only after funding is confirmed
- certificates, publication, mentorship, platform access, internships, or letters must identify the actual issuing organization and eligibility conditions
- do not promise automatic letters of recommendation solely for placement unless the named issuer has explicitly agreed

### 10. Promotion timing

The separate September competition closes October 15.

**FinanceMeta recommendation:** no public launch for the November competition before October 16, and no public copy until the partner brief is fully signed off.

Registration and submissions open November 1.
