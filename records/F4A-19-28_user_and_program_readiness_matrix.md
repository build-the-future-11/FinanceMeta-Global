## Shared participant gate

Before any user-facing session in this group, record:

- host/partner approval and named accountable facilitator;
- target population and why the session exists;
- what data, if any, is collected;
- applicable consent/assent/safeguarding process;
- accessibility/language review;
- deletion/retention policy;
- adverse-event/escalation path;
- whether the activity is education/usability feedback or research.

Do not retroactively relabel ordinary delivery feedback as a research study. If outcome evidence is intended, the research protocol and approvals are separate and frozen before collection.
