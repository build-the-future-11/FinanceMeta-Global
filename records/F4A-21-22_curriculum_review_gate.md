# F4A-21 / F4A-22 — curriculum and facilitator review gate

**Current artifact:** English review draft with 12 lessons, 24 draft assessment items, a practical capstone and teach-back rubric. The material explicitly uses fictional exercises and says it is not yet an approved programme.

## Content review

A reviewer must check every module for:

1. mathematical/factual correctness;
2. distinction between general education and personal financial advice;
3. examples that do not assume a single household, banking system or legal regime;
4. local terminology/currency adaptation points;
5. accessibility and low-bandwidth/paper usability;
6. unnecessary collection of real household/business/banking information;
7. clear answer key and ambiguity review for both draft questions per module;
8. statements that require local qualified review (tax, benefits, insurance, consumer law, etc.).

## Assessment gate

The 24 items are draft educational checks, not a validated psychometric instrument. Before scored use:

- independently answer every item from the learner-facing wording;
- resolve multiple-plausible-answer cases;
- record expected reasoning, not only answer letters;
- freeze scoring;
- report missing items separately;
- do not equate a score increase with improved financial wellbeing.

Completion remains a programme definition rather than an impact claim.

## F4A-22 facilitator acceptance

A facilitator can be counted as trained only after:

- reviewing safeguarding/privacy boundaries;
- completing a teach-back using an assigned module;
- demonstrating the example calculation correctly;
- responding appropriately to a request for personalised advice;
- explaining when to refer to a qualified/local resource;
- passing the documented teach-back rubric.

Attendance alone is not completion.

## Research boundary

Routine programme completion and quality assurance are not automatically research. Any attempt to estimate learning retention, behaviour change or wellbeing impact belongs under a separately approved F4A-23 protocol with applicable consent/assent and follow-up/attrition rules.
