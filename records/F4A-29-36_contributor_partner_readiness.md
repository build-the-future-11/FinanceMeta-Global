# F4A-29–36 — contributor, partner and resource readiness

## F4A-29 Chapter-to-Impact Network

Use a bounded two-week lead trial before granting an operating chapter status. Minimum accepted trial evidence:

- one scoped task accepted in writing;
- one reviewable artifact delivered;
- artifact reviewed against an explicit rubric;
- availability for the next four weeks confirmed;
- delivery host/safeguards verified when participant-facing activity is proposed.

A registration, chat membership or country location does not make a chapter active.

## F4A-30 FinanceMeta Research Fellowship

Build briefs only from already gate-ready projects. Each brief needs exact artifact, reviewer, weekly milestone, data/access assumptions, authorship/contribution policy and a clear no-guarantee statement for publication/recommendation letters.

## F4A-31 Skills-to-Contribution Exchange

Every task must state expected time, prerequisite skill, reviewer, acceptance test, data/privacy boundary and whether it is paid or unpaid. Count accepted work, not raw submissions.

## F4A-32 Regional Financial Needs Assembly

Before any listening session: host consent, non-representative sampling statement, recording/notes policy, privacy plan, discussion guide and a defined path for returning findings. Do not report sessions as population surveys.

## F4A-33 Global Partner Contribution Exchange

A partner is counted only when a dated contribution is evidenced. Valid contribution categories may include reviewer, distribution, host, data/tool, speaker or funding/in-kind support. A logo, positive email, introduction or verbal interest alone does not count.

Outbound messages stay draft-only until the campaign/recipient is explicitly approved. No guessed addresses.

## F4A-34 Reviewer & Mentor Commons

Before assignment record:

- reviewer's relevant competence;
- declared conflicts;
- what the reviewer will and will not judge;
- artifact/version;
- due date/capacity;
- review receipt and unresolved findings.

Employer/institution names do not themselves imply endorsement.

## F4A-35 Data & Infrastructure Access Compact

Start from a blocker inventory. For each access route record owner, source/provider, exact use, permission/licence/redistribution boundary, expiry/renewal date, credential custodian and a working access test. Do not request credits merely because they are available.

## F4A-36 Mission-Linked Resource & Funding Pipeline

No proposal or commitment until legal entity/eligibility, authorised adult signatory where required, project budget, use of funds, reporting obligations and conflicts are checked. “Credits offered” is not cash funding; an application is not an award.

## Promotion principle

These projects exist to create accountable delivered contributions. Volume of members, messages or organisations is secondary to verified artifacts, review capacity and repeat delivery.
