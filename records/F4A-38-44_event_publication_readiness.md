## Scheduling rule

Calendar dates do not override evidence gates. If there are fewer than the required evidence-ready artifacts, reviewers or presenters, defer the event/release rather than manufacturing content to fill a programme.

Publication language must preserve negative/inconclusive results, uncertainty and corrections. A translated or designed output cannot strengthen the underlying scientific/program claim beyond its source evidence.
