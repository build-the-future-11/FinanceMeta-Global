# Answer Key and Teaching Notes

## 1. Compound growth

1. Year 1: `200 × 1.05 = 210` units.
2. Year 2: `210 × 1.05 = 220.50` units.
3. The second year's interest is calculated on 210, which includes both the original 200 and the 10 units earned in year 1. That is the compound-interest mechanism.

## 2. Inflation and purchasing power

**Correct answer: B.**

The numerical account balance rises 2%, so it is larger in nominal terms. Prices rise 4%, which is faster than the balance, so the balance buys less on average than before.

An exact one-year comparison would use `(1.02 / 1.04) - 1`, which is about `-1.92%`. Subtracting 4% from 2% gives a useful approximation but not the exact real rate.

## 3. Diversification

**Correct answer: C.**

Diversification can reduce concentration in a single company, industry, or narrow exposure. It cannot guarantee profit and cannot eliminate broad market risk. A market-wide decline can affect a diversified portfolio too.

## 4. Borrowing cost

1. **Loan A** has the lower borrowing cost under the stated assumptions because principal, term, and fees are identical while its interest rate is lower.
2. Inspect **APR** and the actual loan terms. APR is broader than the interest rate because it incorporates the interest rate and certain fees.
3. APR and interest rate are not the same measure. Comparing one loan's APR with another loan's interest rate is not an apples-to-apples comparison.

## 5. Risk and expected return

**Correct answer: B.**

In general, greater potential return comes with greater risk. An advertised or expected return is not a guarantee, and unusually high-return, low-risk claims warrant skepticism.

## Exit sentence

Accept any response that identifies a financial claim and explains a relevant check using the lesson concepts. Do not score a learner on whether they disclose personal financial information; personal disclosure is neither requested nor needed.
