## Pipeline state

Choose exactly one current state:

- `PROSPECT` — identified; no outreach sent.
- `CONTACTED` — outreach sent; no substantive reply yet.
- `REPLIED` — substantive response received; no bounded next step agreed.
- `QUALIFIED` — fit and realistic contribution are clear.
- `PILOT_PROPOSED` — one bounded activity/deliverable has been proposed.
- `COMMITTED` — both sides have agreed to a concrete contribution/date/deliverable.
- `DELIVERED` — the external contribution actually happened and evidence exists.
- `CLOSED_NO_FIT` — declined, ineligible, wrong audience, or otherwise not worth further work.
- `DORMANT` — potentially relevant, but there is no current next action.
